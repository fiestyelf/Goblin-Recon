# Bold Educational Style

## Use When

The carousel teaches a practical concept and needs strong scroll-stopping structure.

## Rules

- Strong first-slide hook.
- One idea per slide.
- Big contrast between headline and body.
- Use numbers, labels, or simple diagrams when helpful.
- End with one action-focused CTA.

## Avoid

- Teaching too many ideas in one carousel.
- Small body text.
- Decorative visuals that fight the lesson.
