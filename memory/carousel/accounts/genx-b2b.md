# GenX Academy — B2B Carousel Rules

## Palette
- Forest Green: #1A3A2A
- Cream: #F5F0E8
- Gold: #B8960C
- Avoid: pinks, blues, bright red, clinical brightness, hustle-bro thumbnail energy.

## Tone
- Results, not advice.
- Operators, not advisors.
- Rigorous, no-BS, science-backed, human-centric, provocative, grounded.
- No generic consulting filler or advice-merchant language.

## Slide Preferences
- Minimal, proof-forward, premium, structural.
- Strong typography, restrained gold accents, no decoration for decoration's sake.
- Prefer frameworks, evidence, tradeoffs, and implementation takeaways.
- Facebook and LinkedIn variants should be direct and readable.

## Blacklist Watch
Use `config/brand-voice.yaml` before final copy. Remove corporate filler, hype, fake urgency, and generic authority claims.
