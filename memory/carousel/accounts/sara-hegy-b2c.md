# Dr. Sara Hegy — B2C Carousel Rules

## Palette
- Clay: #C4A882
- Espresso: #2C2420
- Gold: #D4956A
- Cream: #F5F0E8
- Avoid: pinks, blues, bright red, clinical brightness, stock-photo cheesiness.

## Tone
- Real science and real soul.
- Warm, playful, reflective, emotionally true.
- Depth plus play; never precious, solemn, or woo.
- Transformation language only when backed by specific before/after proof or real client language.

## Slide Preferences
- Cinematic, warm, lamplit, editorial, typography-led.
- Use film-grain warmth and grounded human texture.
- Hook slides can use curiosity/tension; body slides need clarity and breathing room.
- Avoid generic coaching funnel visuals.

## Blacklist Watch
Use `config/brand-voice.yaml` before final copy. Auto-fix or shelve copy using woo, fake urgency, or empty pleasing phrases.
