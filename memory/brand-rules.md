# GenX Brand Rules

This is the operational brand memory for Goblin Recon. It condenses local-only internal brand source material into rules the agents must load before trend scans, source hunting, moment finding, competitor scouting, and brief generation. Do not commit raw founder/client discovery notes; keep only sanitized operational rules here.

## Read First

- [RULE] `X` does not mean Generation X. `X` means the unknown factor: the currently invisible variable that creates balance, fulfillment, and next-level results.
- [RULE] Treat B2C and B2B as separate front doors with separate audiences, voice, funnel, and visual worlds.
- [RULE] All brand-facing marketing and content is English only. Delivery may flex for Arabic-speaking clients, but outward brand content does not speak Arabic.
- [RULE] Do not guess open founder decisions. Flag them for Sara.

## Mission Spine

- Break the inherited belief that a big life is not available to "people like us."
- Democratize opportunity without hype or empty promises.
- Make the brand living proof that scarcity stories can be broken.
- B2C clients are unlocked, not built from scratch: a dimmed flame, not no flame.
- B2B clients need operators who have built, not advisors who only advise.

## B2C Rules

- [RULE] B2C sells experiential transformation: presence, emotional clarity, aliveness, and the feeling of being truly seen.
- [RULE] B2C voice is alive, awakening, limitless, soulful-but-grounded.
- [RULE] Depth plus play. Never depth plus preciousness.
- [RULE] Never solemn, hushed, reverent, self-serious, floaty, or above-the-clouds.
- [RULE] Avoid tactical advice content. The brand is transformation, not information.
- [RULE] No PDFs or advice-guide lead magnets. Use a felt diagnostic/quiz tied to the X factor instead.

## B2B Rules

- [RULE] B2B voice is rigorous, no-BS, science-backed, human-centric, powerful, and structurally clear.
- [RULE] Keep the provocative edge. It qualifies the right client and filters the wrong one.
- [RULE] Never sound like an advice merchant saying "you should be doing this."
- [RULE] B2B positioning: practical implementation, operational results, hands dirty, operators not advisors.
- [RULE] LinkedIn is credibility/visibility only. B2B deals close through warm intros, referrals, rooms, and networks.

## Audience Profiles

### B2C Ideal Customer

- Ages 31-55, men and women.
- Successful on paper but disconnected from spark, energy, aliveness, and purpose.
- Often senior professionals, first-time managers, expats, or third-culture professionals.
- Core question: "What is next for me? How can I feel connected, energetic, and alive again so I can do what my heart actually wants?"
- Key objection: money uncertainty and family-first guilt. Reframe as cycle-breaking, not discount urgency.

### B2B Ideal Buyer

- SME owner with 50-200 employees, often burnt out from micromanaging, bad hiring, broken operations, resource gaps, or AI integration pressure.
- Wants senior team to stop bringing every problem and wants only major updates, wins, and big decisions.
- Speaking buyer: CEO or conference/event organizer seeking an esteemed, inspirational speaker.
- Key objection: Sara is young and the promise feels broad. Neutralize with Dr. title, Wikipedia/media proof, track record, and operator framing.

## Content Rules

- [RULE] 3 posts/day total output is the goal only when quality clears the brand bar.
- [RULE] Repurpose from Sara sessions, talks, meditations, testimonials, and client language instead of forcing founder churn.
- [RULE] Session-derived content must be anonymized and client-consented.
- What works: provocative reframes, emotional story, coined language, depth plus play, real before/after.
- What fails: tactical step-by-step advice content, generic leadership tips, over-polished heavy content with too little warmth.
- Celebration content is authority/reach fuel, not the main conversion engine.

## Voice Calibration

- Admired qualities: clarity without dumbing down, substance over gloss, boldness, authenticity, wisdom, earned authority.
- Cringe signals: fakeness, hype with no value, lack of integrity, empty promises, advice not born from real experience, pretense, fake urgency.
- Screen against manufactured urgency and promise/delivery mismatch.

## Word And Phrase Blacklist

The blacklist is enforced in `config/brand-voice.yaml`. Any brief using these terms must be rewritten or shelved unless the term appears only in a quoted source transcript and is clearly not GenX copy.

### Hard No: Hype, Hustle, Fake Urgency

game-changer, disrupt, crush it, 10x, level up, beast mode, grind, rise and grind, hustle, no excuses, last chance, act now, don't miss out, secret formula, hack, life hack, growth hack, the one weird trick, blueprint, cheat code, ninja, rockstar, guru, escape the 9-to-5, get rich, overnight success, explode your, skyrocket, supercharge, turbocharge, unlock the secrets.

### Hard No: Corporate Or Consultant Filler

synergy, leverage as a verb, circle back, move the needle, low-hanging fruit, deep dive, drill down, bandwidth, touch base, value-add, best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, paradigm shift, holistic solution, thought leader when self-applied, seamless, robust, scalable as a buzzword, mission-critical, actionable insights, take it to the next level, win-win.

### Hard No: Woo Or Spiritual Bypass

high-vibe, raise your vibration, manifest as magic, the universe has your back, divine timing, sacred container, hold space as a cliche, align with your truth, your highest self, light worker, abundance mindset as bait, awaken your inner goddess, soul tribe, trust the process as overused filler, good vibes only, love and light.

### Avoid: Empty, Pleasing, Generic

empower, empowerment, unlock your potential, be your best self, transform your life as empty filler, passionate about, journey as overused filler, top-notch, amazing, incredible, revolutionary, ultimate, no-brainer, results-driven, dynamic, take the leap, invest in yourself as pressure.

## Nuance Words

- `limitless`, `alive`, `awakening`, and `transform` are part of the brand DNA.
- They are allowed only when backed by a specific before/after, real client language, or concrete transformation.
- Never use them as empty motivational filler.

## Visual Rules

- B2B: deep/forest green base, restrained gold accent, grounded, intelligent, premium, proof-forward, zero decoration.
- B2C: warm earth tones, terracotta/clay, cream/sand, golden warmth, deep warm charcoal/espresso.
- [RULE] No pinks, blues, clinical brightness, bright corporate blue/red, stock-photo cheesiness, hustle-bro thumbnail energy, or generic coaching funnel look.
