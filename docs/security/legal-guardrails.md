# Legal and Platform Guardrails

Goblin Recon is a research and briefing tool. It should not be used to bypass platform rules or republish content without review.

This is not legal advice. It is a simple operating policy for internal use.

---

## Allowed by Default

1. Reading public webpages.
2. Summarizing public news articles with links.
3. Extracting short public YouTube transcript excerpts for internal review.
4. Monitoring public competitor pricing pages, blogs, and changelogs.
5. Creating internal briefs with source attribution.

---

## Not Allowed by Default

1. Bypassing paywalls.
2. Bypassing captchas.
3. Scraping private accounts or private communities.
4. Using personal social accounts for automation.
5. Downloading and reposting copyrighted videos without review.
6. Publishing claims without source links.
7. Generating fake quotes, fake timestamps, or fake citations.

---

## Human Review Required

Human review is required before:

1. Publishing any clip.
2. Publishing competitor claims.
3. Publishing legal, financial, medical, employment, or safety claims.
4. Publishing content involving identifiable private individuals.
5. Using a new third-party API.
6. Running automation from a company social account.

---

## Source Attribution Rules

Every approved brief must include:

1. Original URL.
2. Creator or publisher name.
3. Publication date.
4. Timestamp when relevant.
5. Whether the story is confirmed or unverified.

No URL means do not include it.

---

## Competitor Research Rules

Allowed:

1. Public pricing pages.
2. Public feature pages.
3. Public blogs and changelogs.
4. Public social posts.
5. Public ads libraries where access is allowed.

Not allowed:

1. Creating fake accounts to access competitor areas.
2. Misrepresenting identity to get private information.
3. Scraping customer portals or trial-only private data without approval.
4. Collecting personal employee data that is not needed.

---

## Publish-or-Shelve Decision

Shelve the item if:

1. The source is unclear.
2. The date is missing.
3. The clip may misrepresent the speaker.
4. The claim is serious but only has one source.
5. The content comes from private or restricted access.
6. The legal/copyright status is unclear.

When unsure, shelve rather than recommend.
